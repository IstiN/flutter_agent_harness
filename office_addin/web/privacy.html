Privacy — fa for Outlook

fa is a taskpane add-in for Outlook. The short version: your mail stays in Outlook unless you ask the agent about it, and what it reads goes only to the AI provider you connected.

What the add-in accesses

  • Read/write of the item you are viewing (ReadWriteItem): the taskpane runs on the mail item you opened it on, so the agent can read that message and act on your request.
  • No mailbox-wide access. The add-in never scans other folders, never reads mail you did not open it on, and requests no broader permission tier.

Where your data goes

  • fa runs local-first: agent state stays on your device.
  • Prompts that include mail content are sent only to the AI provider you configured (your API key, your provider). fa does not operate a mail-copying backend.
