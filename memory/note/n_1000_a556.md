# Note: n_1000_a556

flutter_agent_harness rework gotchas (pr-1648 round, 2026-10-17): (1) beyond the known auto-commit/merge-revert behavior, the bot also runs a HARD `git reset --hard origin/<branch>` on the rework checkout (reflog: "reset: moving to origin/feat/1646-antistop") — it wipes staged AND unstaged fixes (git checkout origin/main -- <file> alone is NOT durable; commit+push immediately after every restore, and re-verify edits survived before verifying tests).
